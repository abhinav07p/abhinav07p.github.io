TrialMatch AI – Agentic Multi-Modal Clinical Trial Matching

A seven-agent pipeline that matches patients to clinical trials from text, voice dictation, or medical images. It extracts entities (NER + RxNorm), retrieves trials from ClinicalTrials.gov and a Qdrant vector store, parses eligibility criteria with a fine-tuned BioBERT or few-shot prompting, and cross-checks medications against OpenFDA labels.

Covers five generative AI components: prompt engineering, fine-tuning, RAG, multimodal integration (Whisper, OCR + vision LLM, TTS), and synthetic patient data generation. Every match comes with criterion-by-criterion reasoning and citations, and unevaluated criteria are flagged instead of hidden. Runs in demo, local Ollama, or API mode, with PDF, CSV, JSON, and audio exports.
